<p class="wp-block-paragraph">When you are building or renovating, plywood and blockboard are two materials you will be spending a lot of money on. And yet, most people choose a brand based on what the carpenter suggests or what is available at the local shop. That is not always the best approach.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Here is what actually matters when you are trying to find the best blockboard company in India.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Check the BIS Certifications</strong></h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This is the most basic thing to verify. In India, plywood and blockboard are covered under standards like IS:303, IS:710, IS:1659, and IS:5509. These certifications tell you that the product has been tested for things like moisture resistance and bonding strength. If a brand cannot show you these, that itself tells you something.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Know Which Grade You Actually Need</strong></h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">BWR grade plywood is suitable for kitchens and bathrooms. BWP or marine-grade plywood is for areas that see prolonged water exposure. MR grade is fine for interior furniture in dry spaces.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Blockboard works well for door shutters, long shelves, and large panels. Its core is made of wooden battens, which makes it lighter than solid wood while still being strong enough for everyday use.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Knowing this helps you have a more informed conversation with your supplier, and it helps you identify the <a href="https://www.saburiply.com/saburi-perennial-blockboard"><strong>best blockboard company in India</strong></a> that actually manufactures what your project needs.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Look at What Goes Inside the Product</strong></h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The quality of blockboard is largely about what you cannot see. Hardwood or Gurjan cores hold screws better and are less likely to warp over time. Phenol Formaldehyde adhesive is more reliable than Urea Formaldehyde, especially in humid conditions.</p>

The bonding is done with boiling-water proof Phenol Formaldehyde adhesive. These are not just claims on a brochure. They are the things that actually determine how long your furniture will last.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Ask About the Warranty</strong></h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A brand that believes in its own product will offer a proper warranty. Some companies offer lifetime warranties on select ranges. Always ask about this before buying, and make sure it is documented.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How Long Has the Company Been Around</strong></h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This matters more than people think. A company that has been in the business for two or three decades has had the time to sort out its processes and build a dependable distribution network. When you are looking for the best blockboard company in India, this kind of track record is a reasonable thing to look for.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Sustainability Is Worth Thinking About</strong></h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Where the wood comes from and how it is processed affects both the environment and the air quality inside your home. Look for companies that source responsibly and hold certifications for eco-friendly, non-polluting production.</p>
