<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What is Blockboard?</strong></h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Blockboard is produced wood formed by strips of softwood laid edge to edge and pressed between two veneer layers under pressure. This makes a light but strong material with a fine finish and suitable for furniture and cabinet work. Saburi Ply, the&nbsp;<a href="https://www.saburiply.com/block-board-india.php">top blockboard brand in India</a>&nbsp;provide best quality and longevity in products so blockboard is a best choice for your next project.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Understanding Blockboard’s Composition:</strong></h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Blockboard is used to a large extent in cabinet-making such as cabinets, tables, and chairs since it is strong and silky smooth. Blockboard is the choice also for doors, window frames, partition walls of a house, wall panelling, and load-bearing shelves which will not sag.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Benefits of Blockboard</strong></h2>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Light Handling:</strong>&nbsp;Blockboard is lighter to handle compared to plyboard, therefore suitable for applications where material handling is critical.</li>

<li><strong>Least Warping and Sagging:&nbsp;</strong>Because it has a multi- core, blockboard warps and sags less, therefore suitable for long pieces of furniture or long-span applications like bookcases and doors.</li>

<li><strong>Good Screw Holding Strength:&nbsp;</strong>Blockboard’s core provides it with a superior screw-holding strength, which makes it more desirable to use where hard and strong joints are needed.</li>

<li><strong>Smooth Finish Surface:&nbsp;</strong>Blockboard’s smooth finish surface provides it with the ideal surface for the production of a smooth professional finish.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Blockboard vs. Plywood</strong></h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Although blockboard and plywood are engineered wood products, they have different composition and uses.</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Construction:&nbsp;</strong>Blockboard consists of a softwood core made wit battens, whereas plywood consists of several layers of veneer laminated together.</li>

<li><strong>Strength:</strong>&nbsp;Blockboard is stable and stronger, particularly for bigger panels. Plywood is stronger in any direction.</li>

<li><strong>Weight:&nbsp;</strong>Blockboard is lighter as compared to plywood.</li>

<li><strong>Applications:&nbsp;</strong>Blockboard is widely used for the construction of furniture and internal divisions, whereas plywood is used more diversely and is highly versatile.</li>
</ul>
