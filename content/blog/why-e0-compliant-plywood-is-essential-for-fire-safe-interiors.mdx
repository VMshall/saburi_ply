<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What is E0 Compliant Plywood?</strong></h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">E0 compliant plywood is timber manufactured with ultra-low formaldehyde emissions, which are very similar to natural levels present in untreated wood. Formaldehyde, a typical component of traditional adhesives, is a harmful VOC (volatile organic compound) that can influence the indoor air quality. E0-grade plywood keeps indoor spaces cleaner and safer, making them happier places for your family, school children, hospital patients, and office colleagues.</p>
