<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Frequently Asked Questions</strong></h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">These are the questions buyers, architects, and contractors ask most often, answered directly.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Q: Which is the best plywood brand in India?</strong></p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A: Depends on what you are building. For warranty coverage and certification depth, Saburi Ply is hard to beat, with 10x money-back on Titanium Plus, and certifications covering BIS, CARB, FSC, CE, IGBC, and ISO 9001. If you need MDF, Greenply is the stronger choice. If availability in smaller cities matters, CenturyPly has the widest dealer reach. Know your requirement first. Then pick your brand.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Q: Which plywood brand has the best warranty in India?</strong></p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A: Saburi Ply’s warranty structure is the most graduated of any Indian manufacturer, covering 15 years on Modwud chipboard, 30 years on Gold 710, 5x money-back on Club H+, 7x on Perennial, and 10x on Titanium Plus. Greenply offers 300% lifetime on Green Club Premium. CenturyPly goes up to 50 years on Architect Ply. Of these, Saburi’s 10x money-back is the strongest financial commitment any Indian plywood manufacturer has put in writing.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Q: Is Saburi Ply CARB certified?</strong></p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A: Yes. CARB stands for California Air Resources Board, and it sets formaldehyde emission limits that are stricter than most Indian standards. Saburi holds this certification and goes a step further with E0 compliance, which sits above even the E0 standard. CenturyPly and Austin Plywood also hold CARB certification among the top 10 Indian manufacturers. It is worth checking directly with each brand for your specific product requirement.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Q: What is the most eco-friendly plywood brand in India?</strong></p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A: Saburi Ply carries FSC certification, IGBC recognition, E0 emission compliance, and makes Neowud, a panel product made from rice husk waste with a UL94 V0 fire rating and zero formaldehyde emissions. Greenply is a genuine alternative, FSC certified, IGBC recognised, and E0 compliant. Both qualify for LEED credit documentation. Either is a sound choice for a green building project.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Q: What is the difference between MR, BWR, and BWP plywood?</strong></p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A: Three grades. Three different jobs. MR (Moisture Resistant) is for dry indoor spaces, bedrooms, living rooms, study furniture. BWR (Boiling Water Resistant) handles kitchens and bathrooms where humidity is a daily reality. BWP or Marine plywood (IS:710) is for sustained water exposure, boats, outdoor structures, coastal construction. Using the wrong grade for the wrong space is one of the most common and costly plywood mistakes. Saburi makes all three under certified BIS standards.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Q: What is QuadPro technology in Saburi Ply?</strong></p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A: Most plywood relies on a single adhesive layer between veneers. QuadPro is Saburi’s proprietary system that engineers bonding at multiple points across the veneer layers. The practical result is more consistent load-bearing strength across the full panel and lower risk of delamination over time. It is not a marketing term, it is how the panel is built.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Q: How long does Saburi Fire Retardant Ply resist fire?</strong></p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A: Saburi’s FR Ply is certified to IS:5509. It comes in multiple variants based on the level of fire protection your project needs, and the rating determines how long the panel resists flame penetration. It also produces less smoke than standard plywood. In a fire, smoke is often more dangerous than the flame itself. That lower smoke generation is not a side feature, it is part of why this product gets specified for hospitals, schools, theatres, and restaurants.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Q: What is NRFC plywood and who makes it in India?</strong></p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A: NRFC stands for Natural Reinforced Fibre Composite. It is a panel product made from rice husk agricultural waste instead of timber. Saburi’s Neowud is the only NRFC product from a top-10 Indian plywood manufacturer. It carries a UL94 V0 fire rating and zero formaldehyde emission. For projects targeting LEED certification, speak to Saburi’s team about documentation support.</p>
