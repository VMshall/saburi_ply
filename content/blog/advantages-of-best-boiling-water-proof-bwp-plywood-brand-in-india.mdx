<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What is&nbsp; BWP Plywood?</strong></h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">BWP plywood, or Boiling Water-Proof plywood, is high-grade plywood treated with distinctive resins for resistance against water and humidity. BWP plywood is produced through the use of phenol-formaldehyde adhesive, which imparts to it its waterproofing properties. BWP plywood is utilized in applications where exposure to moisture is of intense nature, and regular plywood will not be competent enough. All this makes the BWP plywood as the best quality of plywood sheet in India.</p>
