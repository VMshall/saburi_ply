<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Understanding Plywood Grades Before You Buy</strong></h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Before we get to the brands, let us quickly cover the grades. Knowing this will save you money and prevent regrets later.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">MR (Moisture Resistant) grade is for interior use in dry areas. It is not waterproof. BWR (Boiling Water Resistant) grade handles moisture well and suits kitchens and bathrooms. BWP or Marine grade is the highest standard. It is built for areas with prolonged water exposure. FR (Fire Retardant) grade is chemically treated and used in commercial buildings and public spaces.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Pick the grade your project actually needs. Do not overspend on marine grade for a bedroom wardrobe, and do not cut corners with MR grade in a wet kitchen.</p>
